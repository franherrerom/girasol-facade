Dawn

First light.

The sun clears the horizon. 1,024 triangles wake up and turn to face it.

Morning

Open wide.

Every module opens toward the sun. Open, the skin collects up to 2.6 times more energy than a closed, flat one.

Noon

Full power.

At its peak the whole facade makes 184 kW. Here is how it's put together.

  1. Floor slabs on V-shaped columns
  2. A diamond frame
  3. Rhombus panels, 32 modules each
  4. One module: a 4 ft triangle

Afternoon

Still following.

The sun swings west and every module turns with it. Each one has its own small motor.

180 Wper module × 1,024modules = 184 kWat peak

Dusk

Closing time.

As the light goes, the leaves fold back in. Slowly. Nothing here snaps.

Night

Lights out.

Every module is closed. On a clear day the facade collects about 1,150 kWh. Enough to power about 40 homes for a day.
